Professional knowledge

Buying guides that save research time

Short checklists covering technical questions and common sourcing mistakes.

Buying guide

Read a material test report

A certificate is meaningful only if it matches the product and intended application.

  • Compare company, product name and sample identification.
  • Verify test method edition, result scope and document issuer.
  • Record any gaps against local building code requirements.
Buying guide

Importing building materials

Physical dimensions and packing practices can change the real landed cost.

  • Ask for weights, packaging units and damage protection.
  • Clarify production tolerances and replacement policy.
  • Define pre-shipment inspection and Incoterms in writing.
Buying guide

Fire ratings need context

Individual material labels do not automatically establish entire assembly compliance.

  • Identify whether the rating applies to material or tested system.
  • Compare the installation details with the test specimen.
  • Engage a competent local professional for code-sensitive use.
